1. Project Idea & Vision
FinSight AI is a vertical AI-powered portfolio management platform designed to act as a personal
financial advisor and wealth manager for everyday users. Unlike generic financial apps that display
data passively, FinSight AI actively reasons about a user's financial position, monitors global
markets and news in real time, and delivers personalized, context-aware suggestions to help users
grow wealth, protect savings, and reach financial goals.
1.1 The Problem Being Solved
• Retail investors and salaried professionals lack access to the kind of real-time, personalized
financial intelligence that institutional investors enjoy.
• Existing apps (Zerodha, Groww, ET Money) are tools — they show data but do not reason
about it on behalf of the user.
• Professional wealth managers are expensive and inaccessible to the middle-income segment.
• Global news and geopolitical events affect markets, but most users cannot connect macro
events to their personal portfolios.
1.2 The Core Vision
"Give every person — regardless of income or financial literacy — access to an intelligent,
always-on financial co-pilot that understands their complete financial picture and helps them
make better decisions every day."
1.3 Target Users
• Salaried professionals aged 25–50 with investments in equities, mutual funds, and fixed
deposits.
• First-generation investors who lack experience interpreting market movements.
• HNIs (High Net Worth Individuals) who want a second layer of AI-driven insight alongside their
human advisors.
• Small business owners managing personal and business finances simultaneously.

• Continuous ingestion of financial news (Reuters, Bloomberg, Moneycontrol, Economic Times)

e.g., "Rising crude oil prices could affect your ONGC and HPCL positions."

goals (retirement, home purchase, child's education, etc.).

under-maintained.
• Rebalancing suggestions: identifies overweight positions and suggests rebalancing toward the
user's target allocation.
• Tax optimization hints: flags tax-loss harvesting opportunities, LTCG/STCG implications, and
Section 80C utilization.

4. Technical Architecture
FinSight AI is a vertical AI application — not a new AI model trained from scratch. It is built on top of
existing large language models (LLMs) using a combination of RAG (Retrieval-Augmented
Generation), agentic orchestration, real-time data pipelines, and narrow ML models for quantitative
analysis.
4.1 Architecture Overview
Layer 1: User Interface (Web / Mobile App + Conversational Chat) Layer 2: Orchestration Engine
(LangChain / LlamaIndex Agent — decides what data to fetch and how to compose the prompt)
Layer 3: Context Assembly (Portfolio DB + Market Feed + News Pipeline — injected via RAG)
Layer 4: AI Reasoning Core (LLM API: Claude / GPT-4o / Gemini Pro) Layer 5: Quantitative
Models (Narrow ML models for risk scoring, price impact estimation, anomaly detection) Layer 6:
Data Infrastructure (PostgreSQL for structured data, Pinecone/Weaviate for vector embeddings,
Redis for caching)

7. Regulatory & Risk Considerations
7.1 Regulatory Landscape (India)
• SEBI RIA: SEBI RIA (Registered Investment Advisor) license is required to provide
personalized investment advice. Fee: ₹5,000 for individuals; minimum qualifications and net
worth requirements apply.
• SEBI RA: SEBI Research Analyst (RA) registration may be required for publishing market
analysis.
• RBI: RBI guidelines govern any product that touches banking or payment data — applicable
when integrating bank account APIs.
• DPDP Act: DPDP Act 2023 (Digital Personal Data Protection Act) applies — user financial
data is sensitive personal data and requires explicit consent, data minimization, and right-toerasure compliance.
7.2 Product Design Risk Mitigations
• Frame outputs as "insights, analysis, and education" rather than "personalized investment
advice" in early stages to operate without SEBI RIA during MVP.
• Always include prominent disclaimers: "This is not SEBI-registered investment advice. Consult
a qualified advisor before making investment decisions."
• Implement a human-in-the-loop layer for high-stakes suggestions in the early version.
• Clearly communicate AI uncertainty — avoid false confidence in suggestions.
7.3 Data Security Requirements
• End-to-end encryption for all financial data at rest and in transit (AES-256, TLS 1.3).
• OAuth 2.0 for all broker/bank API integrations — no plain-text credential storage.
• SOC 2 Type II compliance as a target for institutional partnerships.
• Zero data retention policy option for users who delete their accounts.

10. Glossary of Key Terms
Term Definition
LLM Large Language Model — an AI model trained on massive text
corpora to understand and generate natural language (e.g., GPT-4o,
Claude, Gemini).
RAG Retrieval-Augmented Generation — technique of injecting external
data (portfolio, news) into an LLM's context window at query time,
without retraining the model.
Fine-tuning Retraining a pre-existing model on domain-specific data to improve
performance on specialized tasks. Less expensive than training from
scratch.
Vertical AI An AI system built for a specific industry domain using existing AI
infrastructure, rather than a general-purpose model.
Orchestration The process of coordinating multiple tools, APIs, and data sources to
compose the right context before querying the LLM.
Vector Database A database optimized for storing and searching high-dimensional
embeddings — used for semantic search over financial documents
and news.
SEBI RIA Securities and Exchange Board of India — Registered Investment
Advisor. A regulatory license required to provide personalized
investment advice in India.
DPDP Act Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — India's primary data
privacy legislation governing the handling of personal and sensitive
digital data.
Monte Carlo Simulation A computational technique using random sampling to model the
probability of different financial outcomes — used in goal projection
features.
LTCG / STCG Long-Term Capital Gains / Short-Term Capital Gains — Indian tax
categories that determine the rate of tax on investment returns based
on holding period.
